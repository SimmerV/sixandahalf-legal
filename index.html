The studio

Six and a Half Studios is an independent studio that makes small games for phones.

Our games have no accounts, no sign-ins and no friends lists. Your progress lives on your device. When a game is free we say exactly what that means, and we never make a puzzle harder to sell you something.

We are a small company registered in New Mexico, and we answer our own email.
