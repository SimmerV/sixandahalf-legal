Privacy Policy — Underworld Inc.

Effective date: July 31, 2026

This policy covers the mobile game Underworld Inc. (package com.sixandahalf.underworldinc), published by Six and a Half Studios.

The short version

The game does not collect, store, or share your personal information. Your progress lives on your device. We never see it.

Data we collect

None. Underworld Inc. contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no account system. The game does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

Game saves

Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the game deletes them. We have no copy and no way to access them.

Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, they are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details. Purchase management and refunds are handled through your Google Play account under Google's Privacy Policy.

Optional Google Play services

If a future update adds optional features such as cloud saves or achievements via Google Play Games Services, those features are operated by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, and this policy will be updated before such features ship.

Children

Underworld Inc. is a dark-comedy game about workplace bureaucracy and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from any user of any age.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before those changes take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact address listed on the game's Google Play store page. (A dedicated studio contact address is coming soon and will be posted here.)
