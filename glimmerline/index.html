Three ways to play

Daily

One board, the same for every player, new each day. Keep a streak going and share your time with a card that shows the grid without giving the solution away.

Journey

Hundreds of levels in chapters of ten. Grids grow from 5×5 to 8×8, with a gentler level every fifth one. A clean solve under par earns three stars, and stars unlock themes.

Quick Play

Pick a grid size and a difficulty, from easy to expert, and chase your best time. The timer only counts up. There is no game over.

What it looks like

Made to feel good

Cells light up as the thread passes, each bead chimes one note higher than the last, and solving sends a pulse of light down the whole path. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, so a wrong turn is never the end: slide back along the thread and try another way.

Set it up your way

  • A color-blind palette, with glyphs on the beads
  • Reduce motion, which stills the animated themes
  • Left- or right-hand controls
  • Adjustable text size
